Questions

Does the other person need the app?

No. They scan a QR code with their phone’s camera and a page opens in their browser, already in their language. They confirm the language they speak and tap Join. No install, no account.

Which languages does it speak?

The 99 languages Gemini Live supports, with English and French first. Nobody picks a direction: each phone puts its owner’s speech into the other person’s language.

Does it wait for me to finish?

Yes. Your turn ends after a short pause of silence, so you can talk naturally. In a noisy room, Hold to talk makes the end of your turn exact.

Can I keep talking while it speaks?

Yes. Vorp keeps listening while a translation plays and queues the next one, so the conversation never waits on the interpreter. With two phones, each phone’s microphone and speaker take turns so it never translates itself.

Will it answer for me, or change what I said?

No. Vorp interprets; it never replies on its own behalf. It carries your meaning and tone and adds or drops nothing. When an idiom doesn’t carry over, it writes a short note under the caption instead of changing your words.

What happens to what we say?

Audio is never stored. Transcripts and your voice profile stay on your own phone, with no accounts and no cloud. The server that links two phones only passes translations between them and keeps nothing that was said.

What does it need?

A network connection and a modern browser. Translation runs on Google’s Gemini, so Vorp does not work offline.

Can it replace a professional interpreter?

Not where mistakes have consequences, such as medical consent or legal proceedings. Vorp is for everyday conversations: a front desk, a school meeting, a shop, a visit.
